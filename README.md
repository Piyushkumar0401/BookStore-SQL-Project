# 📊 SQL Bookstore Analysis

This is a simple SQL practice project where I worked on a bookstore dataset.

I created tables for books, customers, and orders, and wrote different SQL queries to understand the data and practice concepts.

---

## 🧱 Tables Used

- Books  
- Customers  
- Orders  

Orders table is connected with Books and Customers using IDs.

---

## 🔍 Queries Covered

### 🟢 Basic Queries

1. Retrieve all books in the "Fiction" genre  
2. Find books published after 1950  
3. List all customers from Canada  
4. Show orders placed in November 2023  
5. Calculate total stock of books (by genre)  
6. Find the most expensive book  
7. Show orders where quantity is more than 1  
8. Retrieve orders with total amount greater than 20  
9. List all available book genres  
10. Find the book with the lowest stock  

---

### 🔵 Advanced Queries

11. Total number of books sold for each genre  
12. Average price of books in "Fantasy" genre  
13. Customers who placed at least 2 orders  
14. Most frequently ordered book  
15. Top 3 most expensive Fantasy books  
16. Total quantity sold by each author  
17. Cities of customers who spent more than 30  
18. Customer who spent the most  
19. Stock remaining after fulfilling orders
